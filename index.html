01 / AI & PLACEMENT TECH

AI-Powered Placement Portal

Full-stack developer

Campus placement drives run on spreadsheets and endless email threads — I wanted to see what a real, self-serve system for students, recruiters, and placement cells could look like.

Next.jsReactTypeScriptFirebaseGoogle Gemini API
GitHub profile ↗
  • Problem — placement drives run on scattered spreadsheets and manual eligibility checks.
  • Build — role-based dashboards for students, recruiters, and placement cells with a shared job board.
  • AI layer — integrated Gemini to power a resume generator, ATS score analyzer, and placement-query chatbot.
  • Logic — application tracking and eligibility filtering by CGPA, branch, and graduation year.
  • Result — placed 6th at the GDG On Campus Hackathon for this build.
02 / BLOCKCHAIN & SUPPLY CHAIN

FarmChainX

Full-stack developer

A blockchain-based agricultural supply chain platform giving farmers, distributors, retailers, and consumers a shared, tamper-proof view of a product's journey from seed to shelf.

AngularSpring BootBlockchainQR verificationAI scoring
View repo ↗
  • Problem — agricultural supply chains lack transparency between farmer and consumer, hurting trust and pricing.
  • Build — role-based dashboards for every stage of the chain, from farmer to retailer.
  • Trust layer — QR-based product verification plus an AI-powered quality-scoring model.
  • Operations — inventory and logistics tracking with a real-time marketplace for direct trade.
  • Result — a working system for traceability, fraud detection, and pricing transparency end to end.
